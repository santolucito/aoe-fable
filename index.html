AGE of FABLES

A medieval real-time strategy in the spirit of Age of Empires II — entirely in your browser.

Your Charge

Gather food, wood and gold. Build houses to grow your population. Advance through the Dark, Feudal and Castle ages. Raise an army and destroy the enemy Town Center before they destroy yours.

Commands

Left click / drag — select  ·  Right click — move, gather, attack, build
A + click — attack-move  ·  S — stop  ·  Esc — cancel
WASD / arrows / screen edge — pan  ·  wheel — zoom
Ctrl+1–5 — set group  ·  1–5 — recall  ·  H — Town Center
